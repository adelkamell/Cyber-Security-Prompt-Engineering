# Prompt Engineering for Ethical Hackers & Security Pros

**Turning LLMs into research assistants, malware analysts, and legal attack simulators**

---

## What is this repository?

A complete, hands-on guide to prompt engineering in cybersecurity. It teaches white-hat professionals with legal contracts how to extract precise, technical answers from models like ChatGPT, Claude, or open-source LLMs — without hitting ethical refusal walls.

## What can you do after reading this repo?

- **Understand model mindset**: Learn why models block security requests and how to gain their trust through legal context, not shady jailbreaks.
- **Write professional prompts**: Use defensive framing, persona splitting, CVE references, and chain-of-thought to obtain payloads, PoCs, and analysis.
- **Master ethical shortcuts**: Practical, tested methods to unlock strict models (e.g., Claude) without violating terms of service.
- **Choose the right model**: Compare GPT-4o, Claude, and open-source models in security scenarios and pick the best tool for your job.
- **Get a golden prompt template**: A ready-to-use template for pentests, bug bounties, and educational simulations.

> All content follows legal boundaries, professional ethics, and white-hat contracts.
